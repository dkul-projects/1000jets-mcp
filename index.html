    Safety & compliance

    What "verified operator" actually means

    Four independent checks, re-validated annually. An operator that lapses on any one is removed from inventory the same day.

    ①

    Operator vetting

    FAA Part 135 certificate verified at source, plus IS-BAO registration and a Wyvern or ARGUS third-party audit on file.

    ②

    Aircraft maintenance

    Last inspection date and maintenance programme (CAMP, ProParts) shown per tail, as reported and attested by the operator.

    ③

    Insurance & liability

    Minimum $100M combined single limit liability per flight, certificate of insurance validated before a tail enters inventory.

    ④

    Crew standards

    Operators must enforce FAA-certified crews, recurrent simulator training and background screening under their Part 135 certificate.

    Empty legs

    Why some flights cost half

    An empty leg is a repositioning flight — the aircraft has to fly that route anyway to reach its next booked passenger. You are buying unused capacity, not a reduced service.

    How we find them

    Our scheduler watches operator fleet movements continuously and surfaces repositioning windows the moment they appear — often 24–72 hours before departure, which is exactly when legacy brokers are still returning your first call.
